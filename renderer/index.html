About

Sefaria Lens is an independent study tool. Texts, translations, links, and lexicon entries come from the Sefaria API and remain under the license of each version.

Transliteration is generated locally by a rules engine modelling three reading traditions. It is a study aid, not an authoritative vocalization: stress and a minority of qamatz-qatan and shva cases are approximate. Where a source marks qamatz qatan, the engine follows the source.
